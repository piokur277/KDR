6.11.5.2 Service equipment and handling devices
6.11.5.2.1 _ Filling and discharge devices shall be so constructed as to be protected against damage during carriage and
handling. The filling and discharge devices shall be secured against unintended opening.
6.11.5.2.2 _ Slings of the flexible bulk container, if fitted, shall withstand pressure and dynamic forces, which can appear
in normal conditions of handling and carriage.
