4.1.1.21.6 _ Assimilation list
In the following table (assimilation list) dangerous substances are listed in the numerical order of their UN
numbers. As a rule, each line deals with a dangerous substance, single entry or collective entry covered by
a specific UN number. However, several consecutive lines may be used for the same UN number, if sub-
stances belonging to the same UN number have different names (e.g. individual isomers of a group of sub-
stances)
