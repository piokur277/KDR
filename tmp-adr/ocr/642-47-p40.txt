6.1.2 Code for designating types of packagings
6.1.2.1 The code consists of:
(a) _ An Arabic numeral indicating the kind of packaging, e.g. drum, jerrican, ete., followed by;
(b)  Acapital letter(s) in Latin characters indicating the nature of the material, e.g. steel, wood, etc.,
followed where necessary by;
(c) _ An Arabic numeral indicating the category of packaging within the kind to which the packaging
belongs.
