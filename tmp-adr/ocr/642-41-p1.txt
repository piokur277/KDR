372 This entry applies to asymmetric capacitors with an energy storage capacity greater than 0.3 Wh.
Capacitors with an energy storage capacity of 0.3 Wh or less are not subject to ADR.
Energy storage capacity means the energy stored in a capacitor, as calculated according to the
following equation,
Wh = I/2CN(Ur*-Uu?) * (1/3600),
using the nominal capacitance (CN), rated voltage (UR) and rated lower limit voltage (U).
